About Me

longlostforgotten
Your Name Here

I write things here. Essays mostly, takes sometimes, fragments when I can't finish a thought. No particular subject. I'm interested in cities at night, the way memory distorts things, music that sounds like weather, and objects that outlast their context.

Based in Atlanta. Developer by trade. Collector of things that don't fit anywhere specific.

This site has been running since 2014 under various names and intentions. The current version is the most honest version — which is to say, it doesn't pretend to be anything other than a place I write.
